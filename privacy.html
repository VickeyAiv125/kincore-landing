Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Kincore helps families build private family trees and preserve shared memories. We respect your privacy and process personal data only to provide and improve the service.

Information we collect

Account details (name, email), family tree data you enter, photos and media you upload, and basic usage logs for security and support.

How we use information

To operate your family space, authenticate members, deliver invitations, and maintain platform security. We do not sell personal data.

Family-controlled access

Each family space is private. Family admins control membership, roles, and visibility settings for profiles and media.
