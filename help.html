Help/FAQ

Answers For Families Using Kincore

Start here for common questions. If you still need help, send an enquiry below.

How Do I Create A Family?

Open Login/Sign Up, create your account, then start a new family space. You can add the first relatives and grow the tree from there.

How Do I Join An Existing Family?

Use an invite link or code from a family admin. You can also search for yourself if a relative has already added your profile.

Is My Family Information Private?

Yes. Each family space is private. Admins control who can view, contribute, or manage information. Kincore is not a public social feed.

How Do Invites And Profile Claims Work?

Admins invite relatives to claim their profile. The relative reviews the invite, joins the family space, and can then add stories, photos, and details.

Where Do I Sign In?

Family members sign in through the Kincore app at uat-app.kincore.com. The admin portal is for business owners, not family accounts.
