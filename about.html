A Private Home For Every Family Story

Kincore is a family tree and legacy platform built so relatives can stay connected, preserve memories, and pass on their history with care.

What We Believe

A family is more than names on a chart. Photos, celebrations, relationships, and the people who came before us deserve a place that is private, lasting, and easy to share with the people who belong.

What We Build

Families can create a private space, build their tree, enrich member profiles, store stories and media, and invite relatives to contribute. Family admins keep control over access, membership, and visibility.

Who It Is For

Kincore is for families who want one trusted place to grow their lineage — whether you are starting with a few close relatives or gathering an extended family across generations.
