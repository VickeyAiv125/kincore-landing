How It Works

Begin With One Face. Invite The Rest Of The Table.

Start a family space, place the people you love on the tree, and ask relatives to bring the photographs and stories only they still hold.

01

Open A Family Home

Create a space, accept an invite, or find the tree someone already started for you.

02

Place The People

Add parents, spouses, and children, then hang portraits, dates, and memories on each name.

03

Ask Relatives To Bring Stories

Invite cousins and elders to claim their place and add the photographs only they still keep.

Roles

Who Can Do What In A Family Space

Every family space has roles that control access, editing, and stewardship. Higher roles can manage membership and settings; members and guests participate within what the family allows.

Family space roles
Role 中文 Explanation
Family Owner 家族所有者 Creates and owns the family space. Highest authority over membership, settings, ownership transfer, and overall governance.
Family Admin 家族管理员 Manages members, approvals, and day-to-day family settings on behalf of the owner.
Co-Admin 共同管理员 Shares admin duties with delegated authority — helps run the space without replacing the owner.
Editor 内容/活动编辑 Creates and updates family content and events — posts, stories, and activities the family chooses to share.
Branch Admin 支系管理员 Stewards a branch or lineage segment — members and updates within that part of the tree.
Member 成员 Verified family participant. Can view shared content and contribute within the permissions of the space.
Guest / Public 访客/公开 Read-only visitor access when the family enables it. Cannot edit the tree or private records.

Levels

Recognition Levels Across The Family

Levels reflect participation and contribution over time — from new joins to long-standing keepers of family memory.

Contribution levels
Level English Title 中文称号 Meaning
1 Initiate 初入宗亲 Newly joined member, beginning to know the family.
2 Participant 参与宗亲 Continuously joins interactions and activities.
3 Contributor 贡献宗亲 Has made clear, meaningful contributions.
4 Historian 家族史官 Steady contributions to family history and records.
5 Connector 家族联络人 Helps connect members, branches, or families.
6 Steward 家族管事 Helps keep order and organize activities.
7 Custodian 家族守护者 Long-term, stable contribution and care for family values.
8 Elder Contributor 宗亲长者 Core member with experience and trusted standing.
9 Lineage Guardian 族脉守护者 Deep influence on the genealogy and family system.
10 Legacy Pillar 家族基石 A living symbol of family spirit and heritage.
