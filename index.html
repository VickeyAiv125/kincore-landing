Family space

Your clan starts as a space — then a person

In Kincore, a family is not just an account. Creating a family space creates the container and the first tree node at the same time.

Family space

A private clan home with a name, invite code, membership roles, and storage for the people who belong to this lineage.

  • Owner and family-admin roles
  • Invite by link or code
  • Branches for North / South / regional lines

Root person

The creator becomes the root ancestor on the tree. That person_id is the starting point for every parent, child, and spouse you add next.

  • Claimed by the logged-in admin
  • Used as target_person_id
  • Relations grow outward from here

Build the graph

Add parents, children, and relatives

Tree growth is intentional: every new person attaches to someone who already exists. The admin begins the chain; relatives hang off that node.

Add child

Attach a son or daughter to an existing person. Creates the person and a parent relation in one step.

Add parent

Grow upward. New parents link to the selected person so older generations can be restored over time.

Add member / spouse

Connect spouses and other relatives with typed relationships so the clan graph stays readable.

Belonging

Find yourself on an existing tree

Not everyone creates a space. Many people discover they were already added by family — then claim their place.

  1. Search Look up first name, last name, gender, or date of birth.
  2. Match See unclaimed people in families that allow external search.
  3. Request & claim Ask to join the space and claim the person node as yours.

Beyond names

Keep lineage alive around the tree

The tree is the spine. Around it, families preserve context that genealogy alone cannot hold.

Branches

Split large clans into regional or household branches with their own admins.

History & media

Store photos, documents, and family history next to the people they belong to.

Events

Plan reunions and gatherings with the same membership that lives on the tree.

Governance

Owners control who can edit lineage, with approval modes for careful clans.
