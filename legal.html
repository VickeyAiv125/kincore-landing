Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Kincore (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides a private family-tree and legacy platform where relatives can preserve names, relationships, photographs, stories, and other family records. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, how family spaces control access, and the choices available to you.

Because Kincore may process sensitive family information — including photographs, relationship details, biographical data, and information about children when a family chooses to include them — we treat privacy as a core product requirement, not an afterthought.

Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to visitors of kincore.com, account holders, invited family members, and anyone whose information is entered into a family space (for example a relative’s profile created by a family admin). Where a family admin adds information about another person, that admin is responsible for having an appropriate basis to do so under applicable law and family expectations.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Kincore, we may collect:

  • Account data such as name, email address, password or authentication tokens, and basic profile settings.
  • Family-space data including names, nicknames, dates, places, relationship links, roles, invitations, and notes you or other members add.
  • Media and keepsakes such as photos, videos, documents, letters, and captions uploaded to a family space.
  • Communications when you contact support, submit an enquiry, or respond to invitations.
  • Billing data for paid plans (handled by our payment providers; we do not store full card numbers on Kincore servers).
  • Technical and security logs such as device type, browser, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and timestamps used for security, abuse prevention, and reliability.

Children’s Information

Kincore is intended for family use and may contain profiles or media relating to minors when a family adds them. We do not knowingly allow children under the applicable digital-consent age in their region to create accounts without appropriate parental or guardian involvement. Family admins should only add children’s information when they have authority to do so, and should use privacy controls carefully for profiles and media involving minors.

How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Kincore service
  • Authenticate users and manage family-space membership and roles
  • Deliver invitations, notifications, and support responses
  • Process subscriptions and billing where applicable
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

We do not sell personal data.

Family-Controlled Access

Each family space is private by design. Family owners and admins control membership, invitations, roles, and visibility settings for profiles, fields, and media. Content shared inside a family space is intended for that family’s authorized members, not for public social discovery.

Sharing and Processors

We share personal information only with service providers who help us operate Kincore (for example hosting, authentication, email delivery, analytics limited to product improvement, and payment processing), and only as needed to provide the service. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Retention

We retain account and family-space data for as long as needed to provide the service and for legitimate business, security, and legal purposes. If you delete an account or a family admin removes content, we take steps to delete or de-identify the associated data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or needed to resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including access controls, encrypted connections, and monitoring for suspicious activity. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use a strong unique password and protect invite links.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update many details in-product. For privacy requests, contact privacy@kincore.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

International Transfers

Kincore may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and, when changes are material, provide additional notice as appropriate.

Support: support@kccdigital.com
